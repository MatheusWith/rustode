what the results measure
  - rustode launch to first frame: from spawning `rustode-gui` (release build, the fixture on its command line, a fresh profile folder, software rendering) to the arrival of the line the GUI prints after returning from its first `RedrawRequested` (eframe has painted and called `wgpu::Queue::present`); the driver's clock, the same one the App's times use. The file is already open in that frame, because the GUI opens its arguments before it creates the window
  - rustode main to first frame: the GUI's own `Instant`: from `rustode_trace::init_from_env` (the first thing `main` does) to the same first present; it leaves out exec, dynamic linking and the driver's pipe
  - rustode shutdown after SIGTERM: SIGTERM to the GUI process until no process of its tree is left (SIGKILL after 10 s)
  - rustode peak RSS, all processes: the largest total RSS of the GUI's process tree in the QA watchdog's 1 s samples (collectors/sampler.py); the GUI is stopped as soon as its first frame arrives, so this is usually one sample taken at the start: prefer the RSS at the first frame
  - rustode RSS at first frame: summed `Rss` of the GUI's process tree from /proc/<pid>/smaps_rollup right after the first-frame line arrived: the memory of the open file and the first frame
  - rustode PSS at first frame: summed `Pss` of the same processes at the same moment
  - rustode 10k inserts, applying: applying every seeded insert through `Workspace::replace` for each insert (a char offset, its own undo step), run by the GUI on itself after its first frame (`RUSTODE_BENCH_SCRIPT`, no key sent); the inserts alone
  - rustode 10k inserts, to next frame: from before the first insert to the end of the next frame (the return from the `RedrawRequested` of the frame that follows the step, on `std::time::Instant`); includes the inserts
  - rustode undo of all, applying: undoing every step until none is left (`Workspace::undo` / `TextModel.undo` in a loop); the undo alone
  - rustode undo of all, to next frame: from before the first undo to the end of the next frame (the return from the `RedrawRequested` of the frame that follows the step, on `std::time::Instant`); includes the undo
  - rustode undo steps: how many undo steps there were (one per insert)
  - rustode peak RSS before the script: sum over the editor's processes of `VmHWM` (the kernel's peak resident size) read from /proc/<pid>/status right before the script: the startup and file-open peak; the sum over several processes is an upper bound of their simultaneous peak
  - rustode peak RSS after the script: the same sum of `VmHWM` read right after the script: the peak of the whole launch including the script; same caveat
  - rustode peak RSS, largest process: the largest single process's `VmHWM` after the script (for the App, the renderer that holds the model)
  - App launch to CDP endpoint: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to its CDP endpoint answering `/json/version`, checked every 25 ms
  - App launch to first paint: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to Chromium's first contentful paint in the workbench page (`performance` paint timing, a wall-clock time read over CDP, minus the wall-clock time of the spawn): the first frame with content that Chromium painted, which can be before the editor is usable
  - App launch to workbench: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to the workbench's editor part existing (`.monaco-workbench .part.editor`), checked every 25 ms over CDP: the QA startup benchmark's "workbench ready"
  - App launch to first line shown: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to the fixture's first line rendered in an editor `.view-line`, checked every 10 ms over CDP; includes Electron and workbench startup, reading the file and rendering it on the virtual display
  - App workbench to first line: the two previous times subtracted, per launch: what the App spends after its editor part exists until the first line shows
  - App shutdown after SIGTERM: SIGTERM to the App's main process until no process of its profile is left (SIGKILL after 10 s), as the QA startup benchmark does
  - App peak RSS, all processes: the largest total RSS of the App's processes in the QA watchdog's 1 s samples (collectors/sampler.py); the App is stopped about a second after its first line, so this is one to three samples: prefer the RSS at the first line
  - App RSS at first line: summed `Rss` of every process of the App's profile, read from /proc/<pid>/smaps_rollup right after the first line was seen (the App is still starting some processes then); shared pages are counted once per process
  - App PSS at first line: summed `Pss` of the same processes at the same moment
  - App 10k inserts, applying: applying every seeded insert through the App's `TextModel` (`pushStackElement` and `pushEditOperations` for each insert, `getPositionAt` for its line and column, all in the timed loop; reached over CDP, no key sent); the inserts alone
  - App 10k inserts, to next frame: from before the first insert to the end of the next frame (the second `requestAnimationFrame` callback after the step, on the page's `performance.now()` clock); includes the inserts
  - App undo of all, applying: undoing every step until none is left (`Workspace::undo` / `TextModel.undo` in a loop); the undo alone
  - App undo of all, to next frame: from before the first undo to the end of the next frame (the second `requestAnimationFrame` callback after the step, on the page's `performance.now()` clock); includes the undo
  - App undo steps: how many undo steps there were (one per insert)
  - App peak RSS before the script: sum over the editor's processes of `VmHWM` (the kernel's peak resident size) read from /proc/<pid>/status right before the script: the startup and file-open peak; the sum over several processes is an upper bound of their simultaneous peak
  - App peak RSS after the script: the same sum of `VmHWM` read right after the script: the peak of the whole launch including the script; same caveat
  - App peak RSS, largest process: the largest single process's `VmHWM` after the script (for the App, the renderer that holds the model)

notes
  - (estimated) Each App time is late by up to one polling interval plus one CDP round trip: about 10 ms for the first line and 25 ms for the CDP endpoint and the workbench (estimated from the polling intervals, not measured).
  - (measured) text hash after the inserts: rustode 35e9dae1; App 35e9dae1: equal
  - (measured) text hash after the undo: rustode 58a6184b; App 58a6184b: equal
  - (measured) chars after the inserts: rustode 1058576; App 1058576: equal
  - (measured) undo steps: rustode 10000; App 10000; oracle 10000: equal
  - Both editors applied the same seeded edits (checked by the hash of the text after the inserts and after the undo, below), but through different APIs: rustode's `Workspace::replace` takes a char offset and returns a result, the App's `TextModel.pushEditOperations` takes a line and column (so `getPositionAt` is inside its timed loop) and fires its content-change events, which the visible editor, minimap, bracket matching and tokenization listen to on every edit; rustode's GUI redraws once after the whole script. The 'to next frame' times end at the frame presented after the step (rustode: the return from `RedrawRequested`; App: the second `requestAnimationFrame` callback after the step), which are not the same event and the App's is paced by the compositor's frame callbacks. Treat the ratio as 'what a programmatic bulk edit costs through each editor's own model API', not as typing speed.
