what the results measure
  - App launch to CDP endpoint: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to its CDP endpoint answering `/json/version`, checked every 25 ms
  - App launch to first paint: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to Chromium's first contentful paint in the workbench page (`performance` paint timing, a wall-clock time read over CDP, minus the wall-clock time of the spawn): the first frame with content that Chromium painted, which can be before the editor is usable
  - App launch to workbench: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to the workbench's editor part existing (`.monaco-workbench .part.editor`), checked every 25 ms over CDP: the QA startup benchmark's "workbench ready"
  - App launch to first line shown: from spawning the App (the QA launcher's `systemd-run --scope` memory cap, then the copy's codium with a fresh profile and the fixture on its command line) to the fixture's first line rendered in an editor `.view-line`, checked every 10 ms over CDP; includes Electron and workbench startup, reading the file and rendering it on the virtual display
  - App workbench to first line: the two previous times subtracted, per launch: what the App spends after its editor part exists until the first line shows
  - App shutdown after SIGTERM: SIGTERM to the App's main process until no process of its profile is left (SIGKILL after 10 s), as the QA startup benchmark does
  - App peak RSS, all processes: the largest total RSS of the App's processes in the QA watchdog's 1 s samples (collectors/sampler.py); the App is stopped about a second after its first line, so this is one to three samples: prefer the RSS at the first line
  - App RSS at first line: summed `Rss` of every process of the App's profile, read from /proc/<pid>/smaps_rollup right after the first line was seen (the App is still starting some processes then); shared pages are counted once per process
  - App PSS at first line: summed `Pss` of the same processes at the same moment
